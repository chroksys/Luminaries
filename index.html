Find a plan for your goal.

Start with the school bill and the date it’s due. If you need UGX 1,200,000 in a year, setting aside UGX 100,000 each month covers the target. Try a different timeline to see what fits your household budget.

Explore GoalSave™

Pick an amount you can comfortably set aside after payday. For example, saving UGX 50,000 twice a month adds up to UGX 600,000 over six months. A regular rhythm makes it easier to plan around the rest of your spending.

Explore Steady

Give your group a target everyone can picture, like buying equipment for a shared business. Agree on the cost and the date you need it, then work out what each member can contribute. That gives everyone a common plan to check against.

Explore Circle

Start with the date you expect to need your money. A bill and a longer-term bond can have different maturity dates and payment schedules. Compare those details alongside the risks before choosing an instrument.
