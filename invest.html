How your assets are safeguarded.

We never represent a product as licensed or guaranteed unless its regulatory status is established.

Pillar 01 · Independent Custody

Segregated accounts, never co-mingled.

Customer securities and funds are held in segregated accounts with licensed commercial banks and central securities depositories—never on Luminaries' balance sheet.

  • Ring-fenced from company operating funds
  • Direct central depository ownership records
  • Audited custody reconciliation controls
Pillar 02 · Regulatory Alignment

Built alongside licensed partners.

We collaborate with Capital Markets Authority (CMA) licensed fund managers and Bank of Uganda authorized primary dealers so every product launches on firm legal footing.

  • CMA-regulated collective investment managers
  • Authorized primary dealers for Treasury access
  • Full suitability & KYC verification standards
Pillar 03 · Absolute Transparency

Every fee and risk stated upfront.

No hidden spreads, buried exit fees, or exaggerated return claims. Every management expense ratio, liquidity window, and risk profile is displayed clearly before you commit.

  • Plain-language fee & spread breakdowns
  • Clear liquidity and maturity timelines
  • Quarterly audited performance statements

How are Ugandan Treasury Bills held in my name? +

Government securities are accessed through Bank of Uganda authorized primary dealers and recorded under licensed central securities depository custody, keeping investor holdings ring-fenced from Luminaries Capital's corporate balance sheet.

Are investment returns guaranteed? +

No. Every investment involves varying degrees of market, credit, currency, and liquidity risk. Rates shown in the simulator are indicative examples based on historical ranges—actual returns depend on prevailing auction yields and market performance.

Can SACCOs, chamas, or businesses invest surplus treasury? +

Yes. Corporate treasuries, NGOs, SACCOs, and investment clubs can request dedicated onboarding through our institutional inquiry desk with multi-signatory governance and quarterly reporting.

Are all 6 asset classes live today? +

Our roadmap phases each asset class as licensed custody arrangements, regulatory permissions, and partner integrations are finalized. Contact our team to confirm current availability for your investor category.
