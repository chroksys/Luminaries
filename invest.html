Trust & Architecture

How your assets are safeguarded

At Luminaries Capital, trust is fundamental. We do not represent a product as licensed, insured, guaranteed, or available unless the relevant regulatory status has been established and substantiated.

Independent Custody

Customer securities and funds are held in segregated accounts with licensed commercial banks and central securities depositories, never on Luminaries' balance sheet.

Regulatory Alignment

We operate in collaboration with licensed Capital Markets Authority (CMA) fund managers and Bank of Uganda authorized primary dealers.

Absolute Transparency

Every applicable fee, management expense ratio, withdrawal timeframe, and potential market risk is stated in plain language before any commitment.

Institutional Risk & Suitability Disclosure

Investment products involve different levels of risk, liquidity, fees, and potential return. Capital may be at risk, and returns are not guaranteed. These offerings represent planned capabilities in our roadmap, not a representation that every product is operational today. Launches depend on applicable regulatory permissions, licensed partners, custody arrangements, and operational readiness.
