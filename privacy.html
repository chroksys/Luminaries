Legal & Data Protection

Privacy Policy.

Luminaries Capital Limited is committed to handling your personal information responsibly, transparently, and in alignment with Uganda’s Data Protection and Privacy Act (2019).

1. Who we are

This Privacy Policy explains how Luminaries Capital Limited ("Luminaries Capital", "we", "our") collects and processes personal data through our website at luminaries.capital and when you submit an inquiry to our team in Nakawa, Kampala, Uganda.

2. Information we collect

When you browse this website, our interactive savings and investment calculators run entirely in your browser and do not transmit your calculator inputs to our servers. We only collect personal data you voluntarily provide through our contact form or email communications, including:

  • Your full name and optional organization, SACCO, or group name.
  • Contact details such as your email address and phone/WhatsApp number.
  • Your inquiry category, indicative portfolio range, and message content.

3. How we use your information

  • To respond to your questions about savings plans, investment access, fees, custody, or partnerships.
  • To provide institutional or individual onboarding information you request.
  • To maintain website security, prevent spam or abuse, and meet applicable legal or regulatory obligations.

4. Data protection and third parties

We do not sell, rent, or trade your personal information. Inquiry submissions are transmitted over encrypted HTTPS connections and shared only with authorized team members or trusted infrastructure providers strictly necessary to deliver your inquiry.

5. Your rights & contact

Under applicable Ugandan data protection law, you may request access to, correction of, or deletion of personal data you have shared with us. To exercise these rights, please email christianokitovs@gmail.com or visit our Contact page.
