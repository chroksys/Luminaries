Complete Framework

Six savings vehicles engineered for every financial horizon

Instant Liquidity

FLEX

Everyday Savings

Designed for people who want flexibility when setting money aside for everyday needs and short-term financial priorities without locking restrictions.

  • Instant deposits and withdrawals
  • Seamless Mobile Money (MTN & Airtel) integration
  • No lock-up period or withdrawal penalties
  • Automated balance sweep rules
Automated Habit

STEADY

Regular Micro-Savings

Designed to support consistent saving through daily, weekly, or monthly contributions and a more disciplined approach to building financial reserves.

  • Configurable recurring automated triggers
  • Daily round-up or fixed installment options
  • Milestone reward streaks & discipline tracking
  • Automated transfer to high-yield Vault
Flagship Experience

GOALSAVE™

Goal-Based Financial Engine

Our flagship experience. Define what you are saving for, set a target amount and deadline, and follow a personalized contribution plan with milestone accountability.

  • Target amount & customized deadline projection
  • Automated timeline recalculations if deposits vary
  • Direct merchant/beneficiary payment proof
  • Dedicated visual card for each individual goal
Fixed-Term Lock

VAULT

Guaranteed Yield Lock

Designed for customers who want to set money aside for a chosen period rather than treat every saved amount as immediately available.

  • Fixed terms from 3 to 12 months
  • Strict withdrawal gates preventing impulse spending
  • Higher compounded interest allocation
  • Auto-rollover or payout to Flex on maturity
Coming Soon

DOLLAR SAVE

USD Hard-Currency Hedge

A planned savings experience for customers seeking to organize savings denominated in US dollars, subject to international custody and regulatory arrangements.

  • Hedge against local currency inflation
  • Regulated institutional banking custody
  • Competitive USD fixed yields
  • Full transparency on conversion spreads
Community & Group

CIRCLE

Collaborative Group Savings

Designed for families, colleagues, and investment clubs (Chamas / SACCOs) to save collectively, coordinate shared goals, and build mutual accountability.

  • Real-time contribution visibility for all members
  • Automated rotational or milestone payouts
  • Multi-signature administrative withdrawals
  • Group audit trails and transparent ledger
