Questions savers ask first.

Straight answers on liquidity, Mobile Money funding, group rules, and safety.

Still have a question? Talk to our Kampala team →
What is the difference between GoalSave™ and Vault? +

GoalSave™ is built for ongoing contributions toward a specific target and date (like school fees or inventory), automatically recalculating your schedule if you top up early or skip a week. Vault is built for money you already have and want to lock away strictly for 3, 6, or 12 months to protect it from impulse spending.

How do deposits and withdrawals work with MTN MoMo and Airtel Money? +

You can fund any UGX savings vehicle directly from MTN MoMo, Airtel Money, or bank transfer. On Flex, withdrawals return to your verified wallet immediately with zero lock-up penalties.

What happens if an emergency comes up while my funds are in a locked plan? +

We recommend keeping an immediate emergency buffer in Flex alongside your locked goals. Where early release is permitted on a fixed-term or milestone plan, clear terms and any applicable yield adjustment are shown transparently before you commit.

How does Circle keep family, chama, or SACCO funds transparent? +

Every member sees a live contribution ledger in real time. Withdrawals require multi-signature approval from designated group signatories so no single administrator can move shared funds unilaterally.

When will Dollar Save be available? +

Dollar Save is currently in planned rollout subject to institutional USD banking custody and regulatory permissions. You can register early interest via our contact page to be notified first.
